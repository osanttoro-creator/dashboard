FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before you start.

Does OAZE connect to my bank?

Not yet. You enter your transactions or ask the assistant to prepare them for your review.

Can the OAZE assistant move my money?

No. The app organizes entries and suggestions. You confirm before saving.

How do I subscribe to Oásis?

On the Subscribe to Oásis page: create your account, choose monthly or yearly and pay by card through Stripe. On Android, you can also subscribe through Google Play inside the app.

Can I cancel?

Yes. You can stop renewal in Settings and keep access until the end of the paid period.
