Frequently asked questions

Does the free plan expire?

No. It has no deadline and asks for no credit card. It has limits — 1 space, 2 accounts, 1 card, up to 100 transactions per month and 3 months of history — but it keeps working indefinitely.

Do I need an account to use it?

To try it out, no: the dashboard runs in your browser and the data stays on the device itself. But then it exists only there — changing phones or clearing the browser takes it all with it.

With an account, the records live on the server and survive anything that happens to the browser.

Does OAZE connect to my bank?

No, and that is a design decision. We do not ask for your bank password and we do not use Open Finance: OAZE never has access to your account. The entries are yours, typed by you — and bills that repeat every month you add only once, as recurring items.

If I go back to the free plan, do I lose my data?

No. Nothing is deleted when you change plans. Anything over the new plan's limit becomes read-only: you keep seeing it and choosing what to keep active — you just cannot create more until you are within the limit.

Is my financial data used to train AI?

No. UGLEZ queries go through OAZE's server and use the OpenAI API only to generate the answer. The screen shows which categories of data go along before sending, and no transaction description is included.

Why start here.

  • Semente, with no deadline. The Semente plan is free and never expires.
  • No card to get started. No payment details at sign-up.
  • Nothing to install. It opens in the browser on your phone and your computer.
  • Try before you commit. The dashboard works before you sign up.

Privacy

Your statement doesn't become a product. Each account sees only itself, by a rule in the database itself. OAZE never asks for your bank password. UGLEZ receives only totals, never the description of your transactions.

Accessibility

Readable for people with low vision. Text reaches up to 15.3:1 contrast, 2× the highest level in the standard. Anyone who asks their device for less motion gets a still page. Every control shows where the keyboard focus is.

Lightness

Your month is calculated on your device. Balance, statement and projection do not wait for any server to appear. Fewer round trips to reach the same number.
