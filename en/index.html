questions? we answer

The questions that come up most about OAZE and about Coco.

Can Coco touch my money?

No. OAZE makes no transfer, no payment and no movement of any kind — it organises the record of your money. Coco prepares entries, suggests recurrences and proposes contributions; none of that changes a balance before you confirm.

Does OAZE connect to my bank?

Not yet. Today entries come from you or from Coco, out of what you tell her. Automatic bank connection is on the plan, but it is not in the product — and until it is, we will not announce that it is.

Do I need an account to use it?

No. The app works on this device with no account at all, with the data kept only here. The account is for seeing the same data on the computer and on the phone, and for talking to Coco.

What are coconutz?

They are the requests you make to Coco: one request, one coconut. Free comes with 10 a month and complete with 200, and the balance sits in plain sight inside the conversation — you see how many are left before you write, not when they run out. They come back at the turn of each month. Confirming, undoing and using the rest of the app costs nothing.

What happens when I hit a limit on the free plan?

Nothing is deleted and nothing disappears from the screen. What stops is creating new items of that kind — and the app tells you which limit it was, how much you are using and where to free up room. Going back to free after subscribing does not delete anything either.

What data of mine goes to the artificial intelligence?

Only an aggregate summary: totals, consolidated categories, goals and forecast expenses. Never the list of entries, account or card names, your e-mail or identifiers. The consent screen shows exactly what goes before the first use, and you can revoke it in Settings.

Can I cancel whenever I want?

Yes, straight from Settings. Renewal stops at once and you keep the plan until the end of the period you already paid for. After that the account goes back to free, with everything you recorded still in place.
