Questions about the plans

How does billing work?

By credit card, through Stripe. You type your card on Stripe's secure page — OAZE never sees or stores the number or the security code. The subscription renews automatically, and you can cancel anytime in Settings: renewal stops immediately and the plan stays active until the end of the paid period.

Is there a trial for Coqueiro or Oásis?

No. Instead of a time-limited trial, Semente is free and permanent — you use the real product, with no countdown, and subscribe when you outgrow the limits.

What happens if I go over the UGLEZ query limit?

Queries stop until next month, and the screen tells you how many are left before you use the last one. Errors on our side or provider outages don't use up a query — only a completed answer counts.

Do I need to subscribe to use it on more than one device?

No. Syncing between devices comes with the account, including on Semente. Paid plans raise limits; they don't unlock syncing.
