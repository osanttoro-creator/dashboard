Support

How can we help

Most of what people write in about can be solved in two clicks. It's all below.

Write to us

Tell us what to call you.

This is where we reply.

Check your email address: it is where we will reply.

Write a little more — at least one sentence.

We reply within 2 business days. Requests related to Brazil's data protection law (LGPD), within 15 days, as the law requires.

What to include in your message

Your account email, what you expected to happen, what actually happened, and your device and browser. If an error code appeared, send it — that is how we find the log of what happened.

Fix it yourself, right now

Export all my data

In the app: Reports → Export CSV or Export PDF, from the Coqueiro plan up. It comes out in an open format, right away, with nothing to request and nothing to wait for.

This is also how you exercise the data portability right under the LGPD.

Delete my account

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. We ask you to type your own email to confirm.

There is no going back. The financial records are deleted along with it, cascading through the database. Export your data first — afterwards there is no way to recover it, not even through us.

I can't sign in

Request a link at reset password. It is valid for one hour and works only once.

If you created the account and never confirmed your email, the confirmation link must be opened first — look for the message, including in spam.

My data disappeared

If you used it without an account, the data lived only in that browser — clearing site data or switching devices erases everything, and we have no copy. That is the difference an account makes.

If you have an account, your data is on the server. Sign in to the right account (check the email) and it comes back. If it still does not show up, write to support before changing anything else.

UGLEZ stopped responding

Each plan has a monthly query limit: 10 on Semente, 60 on Coqueiro, 200 on Oásis. Once you reach it, UGLEZ comes back the following month.

Errors on our side or provider outages do not use up a query — if you saw an error, that query was not counted.
